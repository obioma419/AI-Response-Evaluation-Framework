# Evaluation 001 — Basic Science Explanation

## Prompt

Explain photosynthesis to a 10-year-old.

## AI Response

Photosynthesis is the way plants make their own food. Plants use sunlight, water from the soil, and carbon dioxide from the air to produce sugar, which gives them energy to grow. They also release oxygen into the air, which people and animals need to breathe.
